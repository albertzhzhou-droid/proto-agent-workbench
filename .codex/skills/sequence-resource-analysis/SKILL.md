# Sequence Resource Analysis

Begin with the governed materials catalogue. For DNA, materialize selected eligible records before parts search or design validation. For protein, materialize a protein selection and validate and compile it with the protein pipeline. Never coerce protein, DNA, reaction, or computational-model records across domains.

Recompute digests before derived analysis. Preserve accession and revision, source and license metadata, canonical coordinates, alphabet, exact length, and the eligibility and safety decision. Keep searches, motifs, windows, statistics, and outputs bounded. Report derived results as software observations with assumptions and review gaps, not biological function or real-world readiness.
